Product leader, engineer, and music producer building at the intersection of AI, developer platforms, and creative tools.

I care deeply about two things: building great products and making music. My work increasingly brings them together.

I’ve spent my career building developer products and enterprise platforms, with a focus on AI agents, developer experience, and cloud-native systems. I created the [CAR framework](https://yashwanthm.substack.com/p/the-car-framework-why-agentic-ai) to help teams turn capable AI into products people can trust and use.

As **normal**, I produce house music and run Normal Records. I also built **Normate**, an AI-powered GTM and fan engagement engine for my artist identity, along with production tools for audio analysis and mix decisions.

This GitHub is where I build, experiment, and share the systems behind both sides of my work.
